# Strava club announcement (DRAFT, not posted — hand-post by a club owner after launch)

Training for a race with us? I built a small countdown helper called mynextpr (Compatible with Strava, pre-launch).

Pick your race in three taps, add an optional target finish, connect Strava once. Every run you log afterward carries its countdown in the activity description, for example `15 days to go for Delhi Half Marathon. Easy run. Target sub-2.`

It writes descriptions only and never touches your titles. It uses Strava scopes `read` + `activity:write` on your own activities, with your consent. Disconnect anytime and future updates stop.

Race-day runs read `Race day for Delhi Half Marathon. Easy run.` Once the date passes it writes nothing. Questions welcome in the comments.
