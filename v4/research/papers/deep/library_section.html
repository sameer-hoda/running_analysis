Gait-science library

Every score we give is backed by published running research — not vibes. Start with these four.

Land softer: stop overstriding

Landing on the forefoot with a compliant ankle avoids the sharp impact transient of a heel-first landing.

Backs rule: feet_strike — Foot Strike & Overstride
Lieberman et al. 2010, Nature. doi:10.1038/nature08723

Nudge cadence, spare your knees

Nudge cadence up ~5–10% at the same pace and the hips and knees carry meaningfully less load.

Backs rule: knees — Knee drive / stance loading
Heiderscheit et al. 2011, MSSE. doi:10.1249/MSS.0b013e3181ebedf4

Lean from the ankles

About 7° more forward lean takes significant stress off the kneecap.

Backs rule: trunk_lean — Trunk Lean
Teng & Powers 2014, JOSPT. doi:10.2519/jospt.2014.5249

Spot the pathological cluster

Injured runners share a look: dropped pelvis, extra trunk lean at midstance, straight knee at landing.

Backs rule: trunk_lean — Trunk Lean
Bramah et al. 2018, Am J Sports Med. doi:10.1177/0363546518793657
