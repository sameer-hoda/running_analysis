    What connecting allows

    Connect uses the official Strava flow (scopes: read + activity:read + activity:write). Only your own activities are updated, and only with your consent. Your activity titles are never changed — countdowns go in descriptions only. See Privacy, Terms, and Support. Find more races on MyNextBib.

    Monthly/yearly totals in the preview are samples — live numbers appear after you connect Strava.

    • Sign-in happens on strava.com — this page never sees your password.
    • Requests read + activity:read + activity:write only — the narrowest scopes for this feature. No broader access.
    • Reads your new runs to stamp them; writes the countdown into the description only. Disconnect or delete your data at any time.
    • Writes go into the activity description only, never the title, and only on your own activities, only after you approve on strava.com.
    • Your title stays yours. Disconnect stops all future writes; stored tokens are deleted.
