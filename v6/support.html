Support

mynextpr provides all support for this app — Strava does not support third-party apps.

PrivacyTermsConnectManage

Contact us

Open an issue at running_analysis issues. Include your Strava athlete ID (shown on the Manage page after connecting) and what happened. We aim to reply within 2 business days.

Do it yourself first

  • Stamping stopped? Open Manage — check you are connected, updates are on, and the race date hasn't passed.
  • Wrong race? Pick again on the race picker, then reconnect.
  • Stop everything? Disconnect on the Manage page. Stored tokens are deleted; already-written descriptions stay unless you edit them on Strava.
  • Delete everything? Use “Delete my data” on the Manage page (or POST /api/delete-account). See Privacy — deleting your data.
  • Download your data? GET /api/export while connected.
  • Strava-side revoke? Remove the app at Strava app settings — your data here is deleted automatically.

Report a privacy or security issue

Open an issue at running_analysis issues with “Privacy” or “Security” in the title and details. Security issues are triaged first.
