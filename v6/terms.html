What mynextpr does

After you connect Strava and pick a race, mynextpr writes a countdown block into the description of your own new runs. It never changes activity titles, never touches other athletes' data, and stops on its own when the race date passes.

Your responsibilities

  • You must own the Strava account you connect, be old enough to use Strava, and follow Strava's own Terms of Service.
  • Do not connect someone else's account, and do not use mynextpr to spam, scrape, or disrupt Strava.

Strava relationship

mynextpr is Compatible with Strava and is not endorsed, sponsored, or approved by Strava. Strava may change or limit its API at any time; if it does, countdown stamping may pause or stop. “Connect with Strava” sign-in always happens on strava.com.

Free use, no resale

mynextpr does not charge for Strava-powered features and does not sell or syndicate Strava data. Support and maintenance are provided by mynextpr, not by Strava.

Stopping and deletion

Pause, disconnect, or delete your data any time from the Manage page (see Privacy — deleting your data). If this service ends, all stored Strava data is deleted and Strava access is revoked.
